About

I am a linguist specializing in Tibeto-Burman languages, with a focus on Tibetan (Tibetic) and Rgyalrongic languages. My research spans morphosyntax, historical linguistics, grammaticalization, language contact, and community-based language documentation. I received my Ph.D. from Nankai University (2025), with a dissertation on the morphosyntax of the Smar.rong variety of Zbu Rgyalrong. I was a visiting student at École Pratique des Hautes Études (EPHE-PSL) in Paris (2021–2022). I am currently a postdoctoral researcher (CuiYing Postdoctoral Fellow) at the School of Chinese Literatures and Languages, Lanzhou University.
