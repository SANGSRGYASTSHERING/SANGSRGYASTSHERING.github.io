About

I am a linguist working on Tibeto-Burman languages, particularly Tibetan (Tibetic) and Rgyalrongic. My research interests include morphosyntax, historical linguistics, grammaticalization, language contact, and community-based language documentation. I received my Ph.D. from Nankai University in 2025, with a dissertation on the morphosyntax of the Smar.rong variety of Zbu Rgyalrong. From 2021 to 2022, I was a visiting student at the École Pratique des Hautes Études (EPHE–PSL) and Centre de recherches linguistiques sur l’Asie orientale (CRLAO) in Paris. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Chinese Literatures and Languages at Lanzhou University.
